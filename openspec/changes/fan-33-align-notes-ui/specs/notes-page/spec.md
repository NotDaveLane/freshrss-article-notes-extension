## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Link buttons centre their label

Link buttons in the Notes page's action rows, such as **Open article at this highlight**, SHALL centre their label vertically like the neighbouring `<button>`s, and SHALL be at least 44 px tall on coarse pointers.

#### Scenario: Open article button
- **WHEN** the Notes page lists a highlight of an existing article at 1440×900 or 390×844
- **THEN** the label's vertical centre is within 2 px of the button's centre
