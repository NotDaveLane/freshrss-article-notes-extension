## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Highlight cards align with the panel

In the Annotations panel, the highlight cards' left edge SHALL equal the panel's left edge (±1 px) in the normal and reader views, on desktop and mobile.

#### Scenario: Cards are not indented
- **WHEN** an article with a highlight is opened
- **THEN** the first highlight card starts at the same left edge as the "Annotations" heading
