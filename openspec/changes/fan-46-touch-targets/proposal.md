# 44px tap targets for the in-article controls on touch (FAN-46)

On a phone the note editor, selection toolbar, highlight bottom sheet and Annotations panel had 40px buttons (22px for
"All my notes"), below the 44px used by the Note action and the Notes page. They now get 44px tap targets under
`(pointer: coarse), (max-width: 40em)`, with wider gaps between the colour swatches; desktop sizes are unchanged.
